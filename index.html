LsETH Carry Fund live since 4 Aug 2026

The KPK LsETH Carry Fund earns a carry on LsETH through collateralised lending on Morpho. LsETH is supplied as collateral on the LsETH/USDC market, and the borrowed USDC goes into two KPK-curated USDC vaults. Holders keep full ETH exposure and earn the staking yield plus the spread between the vaults' yield and the borrow rate, accruing in LsETH.

LsETH is Liquid Collective's liquid staking token, minted and redeemed through approved platforms such as Coinbase Prime, Figment and Bitcoin Suisse, as well as traded permissionlessly on DEXes. Deposits and redemptions are in LsETH, on Upshift's vault infrastructure with KPK as curator.

How we manage risk

Automated in a band, alerted on carry, bounded onchain

KPK runs the position with deterministic agents. They act only through onchain permissions and within the settings KPK gives them: when a predefined condition is met, they execute a predefined action. The health factor manager keeps the loan in its band; the other agents move LsETH between the vault and the trading subaccount and propose the NAV. Two exit agents, one per KPK USDC vault, withdraw the fund's USDC from a vault back to the trading subaccount when Hypernative flags that vault or a market it lends into. A negative carry goes to KPK's risk team for review.

How a signal is handled
Layer 1 · automated
The health factor manager and the exit agents

Below a health factor of 1.1025 the agent repays to 1.1467; above 1.24 it borrows back to 1.1927 and deposits the new USDC 60/40 into the two KPK USDC vaults, in steps of at least 1,000 USDC. At the market's 86% LLTV those levels are 78.0%, 75.0%, 72.1% and 69.4% LTV. The exit agents act in this layer too: on a hack or exploit alert on collateral a KPK USDC vault lends against, an oracle ownership change on KPK USDC Yield's markets, or a vault-level alert (a gate change, an owner or curator change, or a share-price drop), the vault's exit agent withdraws the fund's USDC from that vault back to the trading subaccount.

Layer 2 · human-reviewed
Negative carry goes to the risk team

Hypernative watches the spread, the two vaults' share-price yield minus the LsETH/USDC borrow rate as a 6-hour median, and alerts KPK's risk team when it turns negative. The team reviews each alert.

Withdraw agentfunds redemptions

Moves LsETH back from the trading subaccount to the vault to pay redemptions. Hypernative alerts KPK on each redemption, so this runs when the vault's own LsETH is short.

NAV agentproposes the NAV twice a week

KPK's NAV Calculator gives the fund's NAV at every block, and anyone can query it onchain. The Upshift vault records it twice a week: the NAV agent checks it against an independent valuation and proposes the update on Mondays and Thursdays. Upshift co-signs each update, bounded by a maximum change set by the vault owner. NAV Calculator ↗

Agents on the KPK USDC vaultsrebalance, exit, monitoring

Both KPK USDC vaults run KPK's rebalance, exit and monitoring agents, which exit a market on a pause, exploit or oracle change. They act on the vaults, not on the fund's own loan.

Exit agentspull USDC out of a vault on an alert

One per KPK USDC vault, each acting with no human approval needed. On a hack or exploit alert on collateral the vault lends against, an oracle ownership change (KPK USDC Yield only), or a vault-level alert (a gate change, an owner or curator change, or a share-price drop), it withdraws the fund's USDC from that vault back to the trading subaccount.

Onchain permissions
Two parties own the vault

The vault owner is a 3-of-4 Safe with two KPK seats, the KPK curation Safe and the KPK Security Council, and two Upshift seats, so neither side can change the vault alone.

Only the operator moves LsETH

Moving LsETH between the vault and the trading subaccount, and writing the NAV, are reserved to the operator, a 2-of-2 Safe with one KPK and one Upshift signer. KPK's agents call only the two transfer functions, through a Zodiac Roles module on that Safe.

Both sides sign every NAV

Each NAV update needs both signatures and is bounded by a maximum change set by the vault owner. Detail in the docs: Governance and controls ↗.

The fund can grow only as far as the USDC it can borrow against LsETH, today one Morpho market. Aave listings would add venues; they are the Aave DAO's decision.

Risks and terms

Main risks, and how they are handled

ETH price
Holders keep full exposure to the ETH price.
ETH exposure is the product itself: the fund earns its carry on top of LsETH and its staking yield. The loan runs at a 75% LTV target against an 86% LLTV.
Liquidation
ETH falls faster than the position can be deleveraged.
Below a health factor of 1.1025 (78.0% LTV), the health factor manager repays USDC until it is back at 1.1467 (75.0%), with no human approval needed. The market liquidates at its 86% LLTV.
Negative carry
The USDC borrow rate rises above what the two KPK USDC vaults earn.
Hypernative watches the spread as a 6-hour median and alerts KPK's risk team when it turns negative. The team reviews each alert.
LsETH
Validator slashing and redemption delays at Liquid Collective.
Reviewed under KPK's Risk Framework and redeemable through approved platforms such as Coinbase Prime, Figment and Bitcoin Suisse, as well as traded permissionlessly on DEXes. The market values LsETH at its redemption value, so a DEX discount does not move the health factor; a slashing loss does, and the health factor manager acts on it.
RWA credit
KPK USDC Yield RWA lends against tokenised real-world assets, mainly private credit and tokenised funds.
It lends only in markets other curators also supply, with a cap on each market, against collateral that has passed KPK's Risk Framework. Its exit agent withdraws the fund's USDC on a hack or exploit alert on one of those markets, or on a vault-level alert.
Vault liquidity
Thin liquidity in the two KPK USDC vaults can delay deleveraging and redemptions.
KPK curates both vaults and runs rebalance, exit and monitoring agents on them around the clock, which keep allocations within their caps and liquidity buffers. On an alert, the exit agents bring the fund's USDC back to the trading subaccount.
Oracles
Manipulation, staleness or failure of a price feed, which would affect collateral valuation and liquidations.
The market prices LsETH at its redemption value (Chainlink's LsETH/ETH Exchange Rate feed) times Chainlink's ETH/USD, so a thin DEX market cannot move it or trigger liquidations. The NAV agent checks each NAV against DeBank. Hypernative alerts KPK if the oracle has not updated in 25 hours, and an oracle ownership change on a KPK USDC Yield market triggers its exit agent.
Smart contracts and dependencies
Upshift's vault contracts, Morpho, Liquid Collective and the KPK-curated vaults.
Collateral and venues are reviewed under KPK's Risk Framework, more than 150 checks across 12 risk dimensions. A 3-of-4 Safe with two KPK and two Upshift seats owns the vault, and KPK's agents can call only the two LsETH transfer functions on the operator Safe, through a Zodiac Roles module.
